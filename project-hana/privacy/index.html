Project Hana Privacy Policy

Data Handling

Project Hana accesses Google Calendar data with read-only scope. Tokens are stored privately for authenticated access.

Processing and storage

Calendar data is used to answer household questions and produce summaries and reminders. Event details may be processed through configured third-party AI providers. Summaries and notes may be saved in private assistant records. These providers handle submitted data under their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

User Rights

You may revoke Calendar permissions in your Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not erase previously saved summaries or notes. Contact the operator at the address below to request deletion of saved records; deletion is not automatic.

Contact

For inquiries, contact admin@tainoforge.com.
